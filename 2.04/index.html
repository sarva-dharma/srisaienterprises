Frequently Asked Questions

Simple, honest answers to common questions about house plans in Bengaluru.

❓ How long does BBMP Nambike Nakshe plan sanction take?
For residential plots up to 50x80 ft, Nambike Nakshe provides online provisional sanction within 5 to 7 days once drawings and statutory fees are submitted.
❓ Will your cost estimation report be accepted by my bank?
Yes, 100%. Our cost estimations and Bill of Quantities (BOQ) are signed by our Registered Civil Draftsman and Licensed Civil Engineer, recognized by SBI, Canara Bank, HDFC, ICICI, LIC Housing, and all major lenders.
❓ I already have a ground floor house. Can I add 1st and 2nd floors legally?
Yes! We inspect the existing structure, verify column load capacity with our licensed civil engineer, and prepare modification drawings for legal BBMP sanction.
❓ Do I need to visit government offices myself?
No. Our team liaises directly with Assistant Director Town Planning (ADTP) offices and portal authorities, sparing you from the runaround.
